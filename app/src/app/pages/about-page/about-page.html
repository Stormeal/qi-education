About QI Academy

QI Academy is TestHuset's learning platform for people who work with software testing and quality. Take courses at your own pace, follow a career path and keep track of how far you have come.

What you can do here

  • Learn

    Find courses in the Catalog and continue where you left off in My Learning. Lessons, quizzes and notes are saved to your account on every device.

  • Plan your career

    Follow a career path, such as Test Analyst or Test Manager. It shows which milestones you have reached and which courses and certifications lead to the next one.

  • Teach

    Teachers build courses with video, quizzes and attachments. TestHuset reviews each course before it is published in the Catalog.

  • Learn together

    Companies can join as an organization, so their people get the courses the organization has opened for them.

About TestHuset

TestHuset is a Danish company that secures and improves the quality of IT solutions. It helps public and private organizations with testing, advice on quality assurance and experienced consultants.

TestHuset also teaches. Its courses cover software testing, test automation, Agile, Business Analysis and quality assurance, from certified courses to programmes made for one team. QI Academy brings that teaching online.
